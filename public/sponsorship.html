What Your Donation Funds

  • Parts & Materials: Carbon fiber, electronics, motors, batteries — everything needed to build a competition aircraft from scratch.
  • Travel to Competition: The SUAS Competition is in Maryland. Flights, lodging, and transport for 20+ students add up fast.
  • Tools & Equipment: Soldering stations, 3D printers, test equipment — the tools our students use to learn and build.
  • Tax-Deductible: All donations through SJSU's Give Now platform are tax-deductible. You'll get a receipt automatically.
